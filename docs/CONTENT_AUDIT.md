# Content Truth Audit — awscloudarchitect.com

**Audit date:** 2026-09-13
**Scope:** all 21 HTML pages at repo root (`index`, `projects`, 11 `project-*.html`, `resources`, `tools`, `interview-prep`, `about`, `contact`, `privacy`, `404`, `admin`) plus the two affiliate data files (`pool-data.json`, `seed-data.json`) that feed `resources.html`.
**Method:** every factual claim with a number, an AWS service/console flow, a third-party product name, or a code-sample version was checked against a URL actually fetched during this audit. Nothing below is asserted from memory. Items that could not be confirmed are marked UNVERIFIED with what was tried.
**No files were modified.** This document is the only output.

## Summary

| Metric | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Distinct claims checked | 78 |
| Confirmed correct (OK) | 44 |
| WRONG (factually incorrect today) | 13 |
| STALE (was true, now outdated / renamed / superseded) | 18 |
| UNVERIFIED | 3 |

**Headline:** the certification fee and duration table on `index.html` — the single most embarrassing thing to get wrong — is **entirely correct** ($100/90 min, $150/130 min, $300/180 min, $300/170 min all confirmed against the live AWS certification pages). The real damage is concentrated elsewhere:

1. **`tools.html` sells a certification that no longer exists.** "Database Specialty ($300)" was retired on 2024-04-29. A visitor can budget $300 for an exam they cannot sit.
2. **`index.html` publishes two unsourced statistics that are wrong by a wide margin.** "32% Job Growth" (BLS says 8% for the nearest real occupation) and "approximately 32% market share" (Synergy says 28%).
3. **`project-ec2-web-server.html` tells readers Elastic IPs are free when associated.** That stopped being true on 2024-02-01; it is now $0.005/hr unconditionally. Its user-data script also calls `amazon-linux-extras`, which does not exist on the Amazon Linux 2023 AMI the same page tells you to pick — the copy-paste example simply fails.
4. **The July 2025 Free Tier redesign is not reflected anywhere.** Five pages say "Free Tier eligible" / "within free tier limits" on the old 12-month/750-hour model. New accounts now get $100–$200 of credit over 6 months and then close.
5. **Two affiliate links are broken or mis-described**, including one hard 404 and one book whose author and exam version are both wrong.

Code samples are otherwise in good shape (IAM policies, CloudFormation, Route 53 hosted-zone IDs, CLI syntax, HPA/Ingress manifests all validate against current docs). `interview-prep.html`, `about.html`, `contact.html`, `privacy.html`, `404.html` and `admin.html` are essentially clean. A pleasant surprise: the `CodeCommit` references are **fine** — AWS returned CodeCommit to general availability on 2025-11-24, reversing the 2024 closure.

## Assumptions made

- **"Today" is 2026-09-13**, as instructed. Several findings are time-sensitive: SAP-C02 retires 2026-11-17 and DVA-C02 on 2026-12-01 (both superseded by `-C03` versions whose registration opens 2026-10-27), and ANS-C01 retires 2026-12-31. The four `SAP-C02` strings in `resources.html` are correct today and are therefore **not** in the approval block, but they need a scheduled sweep in November.
- **Where the site's own figure is conservative in the safe direction, I left it alone** (e.g. Cantrill at "~$50" when the real price is $40 — over-estimating a budget does not mislead a reader into under-saving).
- **Salary figures are flagged but mostly not replaced.** "AWS Cloud Architect" is not a BLS occupation and no primary 2026 salary survey could be fetched. Substituting one unsourced number for another would not improve truthfulness; the right fix is for the author to cite a source or soften the claim. Only the headline `$150K+` is proposed for replacement, against the BLS median.
- **Monthly cost estimates for the four priced projects were not rewritten except for EKS**, where the stated floor ($75) is less than the control plane's own cost ($73) and is therefore arithmetically impossible. The other three are plausible but depend on sizing choices the pages never state.
- **Image filenames in `pool-data.json` are mismatched to their titles** (e.g. the Cloud Practitioner book points at `/images/books/saa-c03.jpg`, The Pragmatic Programmer at `aws-security.jpg`). This is a presentation bug rather than a factual claim, so it is recorded here and excluded from the findings table and approval block.
